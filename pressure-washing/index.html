Stewart Bradley Pier Services / Pressure washing

Hampton Roads piers collect a film that makes the walkway look older than it is — and slicker than it should be.

Algae, dirt, and gray weathering sit in the grain. Cleaning will not replace a cupped board or a fastener that has worked up. It will take the surface back to wood you can see, walk, and stain. That is the job.

Stewart Bradley Pier Services pressure washes existing residential piers with a process suited to the surface. We are not a blasting company. Too much pressure raises grain, scars the wood, and leaves you with a fuzzier board than you started with. Class A contractor, license #2705160892. Suffolk-based, including Virginia Beach and Chesapeake waterfronts.

When washing is the right call

Green algae on the boards. Gray weathering you want gone before a finish. Dirt in the grain. A walkway you intend to stain. Those are cleaning jobs.

Washing is not a substitute for failed boards, soft spots, or a pier that needs structural attention first. Slick algae on wood that is due for replacement will come back. If the decking is done, look at re-decking or a rebuild on the existing pilings or selective repairs before you spend money on a wash.

How we approach the surface

  • Protect the property and plan access
  • Wash with pressure and technique appropriate for the decking
  • Rinse and let the wood dry before any stain

Marine-grade staining often follows a wash. A defined estimate comes first.

Where this work belongs

Suffolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, and the rest of Hampton Roads — including Lynnhaven, Broad Bay, Bennett’s Creek, Nansemond, and Western Branch. Project availability still depends on location, access, and scope.

How it goes

A photo to start. An on-site look. A written scope. Then we wash on the agreed day.
