Stewart Bradley Pier Services / Pier re-decking

The boards are tired. Fasteners have worked up. The walkway looks uneven and holds water. The structure underneath is still doing its job. That is a re-deck — not a rebuild, and not a new pile-supported pier.

Stewart Bradley Pier Services replaces worn decking on existing residential piers throughout Hampton Roads. Many homeowners call that walkway a dock; this page is still re-decking on the structure you already have. Class A contractor, license #2705160892. Suffolk-based. We tell you what should come up, what can stay, and what a larger scope would actually buy you.

When re-decking is the right call

Cupped or cracked boards. Raised fasteners. Slick algae that cleaning will not permanently fix. Patchwork replacements that never quite match. Those are re-deck problems.

It is not the right call when accessible framing is soft, connections at the piles have failed, or the pier moves underfoot. We do not install new pilings or take on engineered structural work.

What the work includes

  • Remove deteriorated decking
  • Inspect and renew accessible framing where needed
  • Install new pressure-treated decking spaced for waterfront
  • Fasteners and finish suited to the exposure

Rope rail, staining, and selective repairs can share the visit when they belong. You get a defined estimate first.

Where we work

Suffolk, Smithfield, Isle of Wight, Chesapeake, Portsmouth, Norfolk, Virginia Beach, Hampton, Newport News, York County, and Poquoson. On the water that includes Lynnhaven, Broad Bay, Linkhorn, Bennett’s Creek, Nansemond, and Western Branch. We re-deck existing residential piers; we do not build new ones.
