Stewart Bradley Pier Services / Meet the Founder

I founded Stewart Bradley Companies, the parent company of Stewart Bradley Pier Services, in 2016 with a simple purpose: take the stress and uncertainty out of residential construction so homeowners feel informed, empowered, and at ease from start to finish.

I bring more than 20 years of experience in residential and commercial construction, as well as design and construction procurement. My background includes managing large commercial construction projects and developing national procurement strategies for Fortune 200 companies.

That combination is uncommon in residential work, and it directly benefits homeowners. Cost control, communication, material availability, and logistics are where residential projects most often go off track. With my experience I am able to forecast budgets accurately, anticipate supply-chain challenges, coordinate subcontractors effectively, and keep projects moving with fewer surprises.

I’m a proud Virginia Tech graduate and live with my wife and two sons on the water in Suffolk, VA, where the waterfront lifestyle inspires both my work and the care I put into every project. The company’s name comes from my sons’ middle names, Stewart and Bradley, a daily reminder that every project is personal and deserves the same care and intention I’d want for my own.
