Stewart Bradley Pier Services / Pier assessments

Before anyone pulls a board, you should know what we saw, what we recommend, and what we will not sell you.

Stewart Bradley Pier Services starts with an honest visual assessment of existing residential piers. Start here when you want a defined estimate; re-decking, selective repairs, cleaning, staining, and rope rails are separate services. We look at the decking, the accessible framing, the way the walkway sits, and whether the work is a repair, a re-deck, a cleaning and stain, a rail — or a conversation about something we do not do. Class A contractor, license #2705160892. Suffolk-based.

We do not install new pilings or take on engineered structural work. If that is what the pier needs, you will hear it that way.

What you get

  • A walk of the accessible structure
  • A clear description of what should come up, what can stay, and what a larger scope would actually buy you
  • A defined written estimate

Photos help us prepare. The visit is how we confirm. The form on this page is the start — not a contract, and not a guarantee of availability.

Who this is for

Homeowners in Suffolk, Virginia Beach, Chesapeake, and throughout Hampton Roads, including Lynnhaven, Broad Bay, Bennett’s Creek, Nansemond, Western Branch, and the creeks and rivers in between. If you already know you want re-decking or selective repairs, say so on the form. If you are not sure, that is the assessment.

How it goes

Send a photo or use the form. We schedule the on-site visual. You receive a written scope. You decide.
